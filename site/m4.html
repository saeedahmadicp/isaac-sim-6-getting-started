Optional capstone brief: stop before an obstacle#

Combine the lidar and velocity-command lessons into one behavior. Start by identifying the actual scan topic on your scene and checking its frame and forward direction. Write a ROS 2 node that reads valid forward ranges and commands a stop below a distance you choose.

Work through three milestones:

  1. Print the forward distance while the robot stays still. Compare it with a known obstacle placement.
  2. Publish zero velocity when the obstacle is too close, or when the scan is missing or stale.
  3. Test slow forward motion toward an obstacle and show the resulting stop. Repeat from another starting distance and record the smallest clearance.

Done means: you can show a scan, a command, and the resulting motion; explain your threshold; and reproduce the stop. This is an extension brief, not an implemented or validated collision-avoidance controller. The course checkpoint only tests following velocity commands.
