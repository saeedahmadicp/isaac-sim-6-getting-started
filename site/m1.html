Module 1 — Building Your First Robot (project)#

Project goal: a four-wheeled robot you built from primitives, driven with the keyboard, whose 2D lidar streams to ROS 2 and shows up in RViz.

# Lesson Video Diagram(s) Checkpoint
1.1 Building a simple robot ▶ 1.1 ladder · structure m1_build_simple_robot.py

1.4 ROS 2 + RViz ▶ 1.4 data path m1_ros2_lidar.py

Project milestones#

  • [ ] The robot drives straight on fixed drives (1.1) — m1_build_simple_robot.py --headless --test passes
  • [ ] W A S D drives and turns it (1.2)
  • [ ] Robot_Camera view follows the robot; RTX lidar sits on the chassis above the wheels (1.3)
  • [ ] ros2 topic hz /scan_lidar ≈ 10 Hz and RViz shows the obstacles (1.4) — m1_ros2_lidar.py --headless --test passes

Stretch project: add a 3D lidar (Ouster OS1) and a second camera looking backwards, publish both, and build an RViz config that shows the robot's surroundings while you drive.

Take it beyond the checklist#

Drive toward an obstacle, then turn so the lidar sees a different part of the scene. Explain how the camera image and the scan represent the same surroundings differently. Save your scene and one observation you can reproduce.

Next, import Carter in Module 2. You will reuse what you learned about bodies, joints, and drives while letting an importer construct the robot.
